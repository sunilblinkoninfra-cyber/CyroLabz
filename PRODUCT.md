# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical and executive leaders (founders, CTOs, engineering heads, and investment/operations teams) managing fragmented legacy software, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools who want one studio to take them from architecture discovery through custom build, applied AI, and forensic audit.

## Product Purpose

Cyrolabz engineers digital transformation across four linked beats: auditing and mapping legacy systems (Foundation), wiring platforms, APIs, custom software, websites, and 3D interfaces into one system (Connect), shipping governed AI copilots and automation into production (Intelligence), and establishing search, answer, and generative engine visibility (Visibility). Success on the site means a prospective technical or executive buyer understands how the four beats connect, trusts the studio's engineering discipline, and books a consultation or initiates a project enquiry.

## Positioning

Unlike consulting firms that stop at slide decks, software shops that build isolated tickets without system context, or AI agencies bolting models onto broken data pipelines, one multidisciplinary Cyrolabz team carries an engagement continuously from forensic audit and architecture blueprint to production code, AI implementation, and post-build verification—with zero vendor lock-in (clients own all code, repositories, infrastructure definitions, and weights).

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from search, generative answers, or direct referrals, often mid-migration, preparing for security/compliance audits (GDPR, ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II), or evaluating how to move AI prototypes into governed production. They evaluate technical depth, clarity of deliverables, and engineering restraint rather than marketing hype.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten service disciplines grouped across four chapters: Consulting & Technical Audits (Foundation); Custom Software, Website Development & 3D Interactive Design (Connect); AI Enablement & AI Implementation (Intelligence); SEO, AEO & GEO (Visibility).
- Built on Next.js (App Router, TypeScript strict), Tailwind CSS, GSAP/Lenis scroll, and Three.js / Canvas particle rendering; targets Lighthouse Performance 90+ and WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes.
- Open decisions recorded in `Cyrolabz Website Plan The Transformation Story.md`: confirm whether homepage opens on the four-chapter transformation story or the orbital hero motif, whether to retain a single accent colour or go strictly monochrome, and confirm production enquiry endpoint / consultation booking tool.

## Product Principles

- **One continuous thread:** Every discipline connects back to the same underlying system rather than standing as an isolated service SKU.
- **Show the mechanism, not hype:** Concrete deliverables, architecture reality, and production governance replace vague transformation slogans.
- **Own your stack:** Full repository, infrastructure, and model ownership with clean documentation from day one.
- **Verifiable craft:** Real-time performance, accessibility, and forensic auditability are demonstrated in the product itself, not merely claimed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast across light and dark themes; full keyboard navigation; strict `prefers-reduced-motion` support (disabling autoplay/tilt and presenting settled views); static fallback where WebGL/Canvas is unavailable.
