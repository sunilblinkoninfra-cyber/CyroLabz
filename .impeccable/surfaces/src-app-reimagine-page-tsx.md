## Direction contract

THESIS: Cyrolabz's promise—"nothing gets lost at the seams between consulting, build, AI, and audit"—becomes physical through an exposed-spine engineering monograph where every transformation stage is stitched in plain sight on one shared axis, refusing floating dark-mode glass cards, neon glows, and 4-column card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Unbleached cotton rag ground (#F5F1E8) and birch board (#EAE3D5) with deep carbon ink (#141311) in light mode; archival obsidian board (#11100E) and bone rag (#EDE8DD) in dark mode; cobalt blueprint thread (#1B4DFF / #7BA2FF) reserved strictly for active chain-stitch links, focus rings, and the primary dispatch action. Archivo display, Manrope body, Source Serif 4 editorial thesis voice, JetBrains Mono strictly for tabular measurements.

STORY: A CTO or founder immediately understands why fragmented agencies fail at the seams, inspects each flat-open transformation signature (Foundation, Connect, Intelligence, Visibility) along the exposed chain-stitch spine with real deliverables and artwork plates, and scopes an engineering intake directly inside the right folio.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width two-folio open binding centered on a live SVG chain-stitch spine linking the 4 stages; left folio holds the headline, thesis, and active chapter specification; right folio holds the working project-scope intake and stage diagnostic; desktop 1440px fits the open binding cleanly above the fold, mobile 360px collapses the spine to a top stitched rail above the active folio.

FORM: Exposed-spine engineering monograph (adapted from craft-making-coptic-exposed-binding, winning over Candidate 6 architectural broadsheet on both audience identification and product clarity). Seed reference: seed:c7b11e5a412320c1.
